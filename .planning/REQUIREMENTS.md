# Requirements: STARE Monetization Sprint

**Defined:** 2026-05-22
**Core Value:** STARE products ship to paying customers. Everything else waits.

## v1 Requirements

### Brand (BRAND)

- [ ] **BRAND-01**: Creator publishes identity in one sentence across all platforms ("I build sci-fi universes with AI — the music, the art, the characters, the games")
- [ ] **BRAND-02**: ONE primary platform chosen and 3x/week posting rhythm established (TikTok or YouTube Shorts)
- [ ] **BRAND-03**: Mezzo art style deployed as consistent visual identity on all content
- [ ] **BRAND-04**: "What is STARE?" pinned video/post live as the platform entry point

### Products (PROD)

- [ ] **PROD-01**: First product listed on Gumroad with a price (ship rough, iterate)
- [ ] **PROD-02**: SUNO AI Music Pack (10 STARE-universe tracks, $15–49) listed on Gumroad or Bandcamp
- [ ] **PROD-03**: STARE Universe Starter Kit PDF created and listed ($9–19 fans / $49+ game dev license)
- [ ] **PROD-04**: Free "STARE Character Pack" lead magnet PDF created (5 cards with art, stats, backstory)

### Funnel (FUNL)

- [ ] **FUNL-01**: Email platform account live (ConvertKit or Beehiiv, free tier)
- [ ] **FUNL-02**: Lead magnet opt-in form collecting email addresses
- [ ] **FUNL-03**: 3-email welcome sequence written and automated
- [ ] **FUNL-04**: Lead magnet link in every platform bio and content description

### Content (CONT)

- [ ] **CONT-01**: Weekly content calendar running — Mon lore session, Wed track preview, Fri Twitter/X thread
- [ ] **CONT-02**: Every content piece ends with a direct product CTA (content-to-product pipeline active)
- [ ] **CONT-03**: At least 1 screen-recorded lore expansion session posted publicly
- [ ] **CONT-04**: STARE knowledge graph (graphify output) posted as social content

### Universe Depth (LORE)

- [ ] **LORE-01**: BioRit Soul-Trading System fully defined — lore expansion session completed and screen recorded (CRITICAL: most commercially distinctive concept; becomes "STARE BioRit Economy System" PDF at $9.99)
- [ ] **LORE-02**: OrganiCycle Bonding Mechanics document written — 2000 words covering bonding model, bonding moment, Widowed vehicles, Open Frequency yard (CRITICAL: emotional engine of STARE; becomes core game mechanic product)
- [ ] **LORE-03**: Vowel-Vector Physics primer written — 1000-word S.T.A.R.E. Academy textbook entry that retroactively unifies all sound/music elements in the universe
- [ ] **LORE-04**: Hermetic Codex mapped into STARE cosmology — 14 laws matched to STARE physics as in-universe philosophical architecture discovered by Rhodhe in Planet Drostam

### Revenue (REV)

- [ ] **REV-01**: $1,000 in digital product revenue achieved within 90 days (by 2026-08-19)
- [ ] **REV-02**: At least one product has at least one paid sale
