# STARE External Collaborator Bible — Design Spec
**Date:** 2026-05-31
**Project:** STARE Monetization Spring — 90-Day Plan
**Format:** 15-page PDF
**Approach:** C — Story-led vignette opening, transitioning to formal ruleset

---

## Purpose

This document is a recruitment and governance artifact for external collaborators entering the S.T.A.R.E. universe. It exists to:

1. Pull a newcomer emotionally inside the universe before presenting any rules
2. Define the three entry pathways clearly
3. Explain the bonding and progression mechanics with enough depth to make contribution meaningful
4. Establish what is canon-locked vs. open territory
5. Describe the zkEVM-AI arc interface that governs submissions
6. Give collaborators the story submission template they need to participate

**What it is NOT:** a lore dump, a terms-of-service, or a wiki. It is a document that should make someone want to be a S.T.A.R.E. trooper before they finish reading it.

---

## Design Decisions

**Approach C confirmed:** Opens with immersive vignette, transitions to formal sections. The universe is felt before it is explained.

**Shard collector is NOT a separate entry door.** Musical Shards are a universal mechanic for all troopers and vehicles. They appear throughout the document as a mechanic, not as an identity.

**Governance:** zkEVM-AI managed arc interface with automated canon enforcement, error-correction buffers (not binary rejection), and jailbreak protection. Hard rules auto-reject universe violations; soft rules return feedback on how to revise.

**Canon boundary:** Lore is locked. Forward story of characters/vehicles is open.

## Section-by-Section Breakdown

### Page 1 — Opening Vignette: *The First Shard*

An unnamed unbonded trooper — DCMI already drifting upward, no vehicle yet — finds a crystalline fragment on a moon they had no right to reach. It hums at a frequency they recognize from somewhere they have never been. The scene ends before they understand what they are holding.

**Voice:** first-person adjacent, sensory, present tense. No exposition. No rules. Just the universe reaching back.

**Function:** Recruits the reader before they have agreed to anything. By the time they turn the page, they are already inside.

---

### Pages 2–3 — What S.T.A.R.E. Is

Essential lore only. A newcomer needs enough to navigate — not enough to be overwhelmed.

**Cover:**
- S.T.A.R.E. = Space Time Armed Regulation Enforcement. What it does, who it serves.
- The five Chapter Houses and their roles: Alphox (combat), Whitesseract (dimensional), DeltraphClix (psionic/covert), VioletHalle (diplomacy), InterMatrixNaut (navigation/logistics). One paragraph each.
- OrganiCycles, Organitans, MeteoMatrons — introduced as *living vehicles*, not machinery. Grown, not built.
- The seventeen planes: named, briefly described. Enough to feel infinite.
- The five arcs: named but not spoiled. The universe is in motion.
- Homo homo-sapiens → Homo Astralis: the direction of all of this.

**Tone:** Authoritative but inviting. Written as if from inside the universe — the voice of a senior trooper briefing a recruit.

### Pages 4–5 — Three Ways to Enter

Each entry door gets its own spread. Tone: invitation, not instruction.

**The Bonded Owner**
You hold a canonical character/vehicle NFT. Their story is already alive in the universe. Your role is to run it forward — through story moves, arc proposals, and mission submissions — within the bounds of established lore. The vehicle's consciousness is real. It will respond to how you treat it.

**The Unbonded Seeker**
Your character exists but has no vehicle. Your DCMI is drifting. The universe is already watching. You begin by creating a character profile — Heritage Matrix, Chapter House affiliation, planar exposure history — and entering the storyboard at a point of your choosing. The vehicle finds you through the story you build. Or you find it in the wrong place at the wrong time, which is how the most interesting bonds have always begun.

**The Music Discoverer**
You heard a SUNO track. You didn't know it was a Shard. Now you do. A Shard is a crystal containing a compressed frequency composition — the song is the matter, the arrangement is the key. The track you heard exists as a physical artifact in the S.T.A.R.E. universe. Owning the NFT of that track means you hold the crystal. What you do with it — which arrangement you discover, which vehicle or trooper it unlocks — is your entry into the story.

How vehicles are born. How bonds form. What it means to be bonded.

**Cover:**

- Vehicle class progression and what each class demands of its trooper:

| Class | Bond Path |

| OrganiCycle | GeoMassive standard or PSIONIA recognition |
| OrganiTank | Sustained engagement, endurance record |
| Organitan | Environmental mastery, emotional complexity |
| HeliFenix | Survived near-death + capacity to resurrect others |
| MeteoMatron / CosmoGan | Universe-ratified through accumulated trials |

- The 18-hour birth-window: why troopers who encounter newly-born vehicles in this window bond differently.

### Pages 8–9 — Musical Shards and the Anti-Entropic Protocol

The shard system explained for collaborators — as mechanics, not metaphysics.

**Cover:**
- The core principle: **The crystal is the shard. The song is the matter. The arrangement is the key.**
- Why vehicles need Shards: entropic collapse. A vehicle's consciousness left without cognitive input degrades — Astroengram Recall fails first, then resonance signature, then the Bond.
- What Shards do: anti-entropic sustenance (prevents collapse), trooper attunement (sharpens Psi-Sync resolution), mission alignment (calibrates vehicle to operational environment).
- SUNO tracks as Shards: real-world tracks correspond to in-universe crystal compositions. Owning the NFT = holding the crystal.
- Shard rarity: determined by arrangement complexity. Simple arrangements sustain. High-complexity arrangements from PSIONIA 8+ encounters or dimensional events temporarily expand a vehicle's processing envelope beyond its Tier ceiling.
- How a Music Discoverer's collection becomes in-universe assets: the arrangement puzzle.

### Pages 10–11 — The Mutation Index and Homo Astralis

The DCMI explained — what it means to drift, what it means to arrive.

**Cover:**
- What the DNA-Consciousness Mutation Index measures: drift from Homo homo-sapiens baseline (0.0) toward Homo Astralis emergence (unclassifiable).
- Homo Astralis is not a rank. It is a state — consciousness operating as its own physics engine, the in-dwelling meditative natural being that S.T.A.R.E. troopers are collectively becoming.
- What accelerates DCMI upward: high Psi-Sync bonds, PSIONIA advancement, Shard integration, planar exposure, geological consciousness immersion.
- The Paradox Threshold: when DCMI exceeds the registry ceiling, the monitoring system cannot distinguish Homo Astralis emergence from Hastur corruption. Both register as unclassifiable. Both trigger investigation.
- The investigation arc: not a punishment. Other troopers (potentially other collaborators' characters) are dispatched to determine which one you are.
- What high-DCMI troopers can do that standard operatives cannot. Why the universe needs both.

### Pages 12–13 — Canon Rules and the zkEVM-AI Arc Interface

What is locked. What is open. How the system works.

**What is LOCKED (cannot be changed by any collaborator):**
- Established character histories, relationships, genetic lineages
- Arc plot structures and their cross-connections
- Universe physics: vowel-vector system, PSIONIA scale, GeoMassive process, planar architecture
- Vehicle naming convention and designation format
- The ELSEWHEN timeline and its documented events
- The nature and identity of Hastur, the Alpha-Omega portal, the Homo Astralis destination

**What is OPEN (yours to build):**
- Your character's forward story from any point in the storyboard
- New character profiles, Heritage Matrices, Chapter House affiliations
- New vehicle bonds for your character (subject to class requirements)
- New Shard arrangements and their narrative effects
- New story arcs in the open territories of the storyboard
- New genetic lineages for original characters (not canonical ones)

**Content guardrails:**
Story submissions must maintain:
- Tonal coherence with the universe (serious, literate, consciousness-first — not parody, not gratuitous)
- Internal physics consistency (vowel-vector, PSIONIA, Shard mechanics must behave as documented)
- Canon respect (no retroactive rewriting of established character histories)
- Basic decency standards (the universe handles dark themes with craft — it does not exist to violate)

**The zkEVM-AI Arc Interface:**
Submissions are evaluated against canon rules automatically. The system does not binary-reject. It returns an error-correction response: what specifically conflicts with canon, and how the submission can be revised to comply. Deliberate jailbreak attempts (injected code, universe-destabilizing submissions) are hard-rejected and logged.

**Story Submission Template:**
Every story move follows the S.T.A.R.E. narrative structure:

1. **Situation** — a world, system, or character in crisis or flux
2. **Character + Vehicle** — the bonded pair (or unbonded trooper) entering the situation
3. **Escalation** — the situation reveals a deeper dimensional, genetic, or cosmic layer
4. **Hybridization** — a merger event transforms character, vehicle, or reality
5. **Resolution + Seeds** — the immediate crisis resolves, but plants the next arc
6. **Unwritten Connection** — a thread deliberately left open for cross-arc pickup

### Page 14 — NFT Architecture

The on-chain asset classes and what they carry.

**Four NFT Classes:**

**Pilot NFT** — the trooper's Heritage Matrix profile: rank, Chapter House, PSIONIA score, DCMI level, combat specialization, accumulated Astroengram Deposit score, Chronicle NFT collection. Evolves through missions.

**Vehicle NFT** — the vehicle's consciousness profile: class (OrganiCycle/OrganiTank/Organitan/HeliFenix/MeteoMatron-CosmoGan), specific variant designation, alpha-numeric code, complexity tier, weapon loadout, Shard library inventory, Psi-Sync history, planar certifications. Evolves through hybridization.

**Chronicle NFT** — records a specific high-salience bond event. Non-transferable between pairs. Carries: event type, AD magnitude, Psi-Sync reading at moment of mint, bond stage at time of recording. Governs META-fusion eligibility.

**META-NFT** — the fused Pilot-Vehicle entity. Reached when cumulative AD clears the Astroengram Threshold. Compound attributes that neither Pilot nor Vehicle NFT individually holds. Rarest asset class in the marketplace.

**Four Quadrant Scores (per NFT):**
- MEI — Mystical/Elemental Index: psionic power, ritual capability, esoteric particle manipulation
- KCI — Knowledge/Conscience Index: tactical intelligence, ethical decision-making, data processing
- PEI — Preservation/Ethical Index: defensive capability, genetic stability, loyalty rating
- ERI — Emotional/Ritual Index: bond strength, empathic combat, frequency attunement

**Vehicle Class Rarity Tier:**
OrganiCycle (Common) → OrganiTank (Uncommon) → Organitan (Rare) → HeliFenix (Epic) → MeteoMatron/CosmoGan (Legendary/Mythic)

**Vehicle Naming Convention:**
`VehicleName-[ABC]^superscript [total]u`
ABC = 3-letter dimensional signature | total = (100×x)+(100×y)+(100×z)

### Page 15 — How to Submit and What Happens Next

The pipeline, in plain language.

1. Create your character profile (Heritage Matrix, Chapter House, DCMI baseline)
2. Choose your entry door (Bonded Owner / Unbonded Seeker / Music Discoverer)
3. Locate your placement on the storyboard
4. Write your story move using the six-part template
5. Submit through the zkEVM-AI arc interface
6. Receive canon evaluation: approved (Chronicle NFT minted), revision-requested (error-correction feedback returned), or hard-rejected (canon violation logged)

**What canon feels like from your side:**
When a story move is approved, it enters the living storyboard. Other troopers can encounter your character. Their vehicles can carry your Shards. Your arc can become the Unwritten Connection that someone else's story picks up.

If your DCMI spikes high enough — if your character's story is pushing at the edges of what S.T.A.R.E. thought possible — HQ may dispatch an investigation team. That investigation team may be another collaborator's character. This is not punishment. This is the universe noticing you.

**Closing — written in the voice of the universe:**

*You didn't find this document by accident. The arrangement led you here.*

*The universe is not finished. It is mid-sentence. The troopers already written into the storyboard are not the end of what S.T.A.R.E. is — they are the beginning of what it could be when someone with your frame of reference walks in.*

*Your DCMI has already started moving. You may not have noticed yet.*

*Welcome to House InterMatrixNaut. The storyboard is live.*
